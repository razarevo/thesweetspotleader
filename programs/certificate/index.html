What you will learn

Key topics.

  • Wise and generous faculty share what has made them successful in ways that are practical for you to copy

  • Learn how to create and perpetuate a mission-driven and innovative team culture

  • Navigate the dark side of leadership either by predicting where land mines will be and avoiding them or by limiting their damage.

  • Communicate with passion and with clarity in ways the reach your team members so that you can be heard

  • Prepare to lead. What do you need to do to be a successful leader right now while also positioning yourself for greater leadership responsibilities in the future.

  • Make your life matter in ways you never dreamed possible.

Is it for you?

Who should attend.

The Sweet Spot Leader program is designed for those who:

  • Appreciate that the leaders who lead the most meaningful change are leaders who bring out the best in their people
  • Want to be a mission-driven leader who is respected for what they stand for
  • Are facing business and profitability challenges that require leadership insights that they were never formally taught but really need to know
  • Want to make a real difference in the world and realize that great leadership is the path to doing so

Applications are accepted on a rolling enrollment basis. Apply any time!

Certificate in Human Centered Leadership

The Certificate of Human Centered Leadership positions you to maximize the human potential of yourself and your team to maximize your business potential. In doing so, you will reshape your career trajectory.

Through a Certificate program, you send a clear message that you are a serious student of leadership and you are someone who understands that the most successful leaders are the most human leaders.
