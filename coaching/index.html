Why coaching

The decisions get bigger at the top. So should your support.

The decisions get bigger, more people look to you for answers, and fewer will tell you the truth.

Sweet Spot Leadership Coaching gives directors, VPs, and senior executives a trusted partner for that moment. Over six months, you and your coach get clear on what you want to change, strengthen what is already working, and build habits that last well past the final session.

Who it’s for

Built for leaders ready to lead with more clarity and confidence.

  • Leaders who want to show up better for themselves, their team, and their organization
  • Leaders stepping into a bigger role
  • Leaders who have completed a program and want development tailored to their situation
  • Leaders facing a specific challenge that is important to execute correctly
  • Organizations investing in the people their strategy depends on

How it works

Six months of support, built around you.

Twelve one-to-one sessions give you a steady space to be heard, challenged, and encouraged. Your coach is in your corner, helping you see your strengths more clearly and act on them with confidence.
